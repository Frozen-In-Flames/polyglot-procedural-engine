INVERSE CITY IMPORTv3.1.0

zero asset dependence: load a real city file and the engine keeps only the math that regenerates it.

Conversion runs entirely in this browser. Your file is read once and discarded. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, and no source geometry is ever stored. What stays is a CitySpec: fitted distributions and structure parameters. Regeneration grows a sibling city from that math, the same statistical DNA, never a copy of your streets.

Drop a GeoJSON city file here, or click to choose one.
